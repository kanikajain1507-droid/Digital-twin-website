ABOUT THE PROJECT

Digital Twin and Data Fusion for Smart Energy Management

This research project focuses on the integration of Digital Twin technology, multi-source data fusion, artificial intelligence, and renewable energy systems to enhance the management of grid-integrated solar photovoltaic systems for electric vehicle charging.

RESEARCH PLAN

Research Methodology

The research is structured into systematic phases covering literature review, system design, data integration, artificial intelligence development, prototype implementation, validation, and dissemination.

OBJECTIVES

Research Objectives

01

Digital Twin Development

Develop a digital representation of the grid-integrated solar PV and EV charging system.

02

Data Fusion

Integrate heterogeneous data from multiple sensors and energy sources.

03

Energy Optimization

Optimize renewable energy utilization and EV charging operations.

04

Predictive Analytics

Apply AI-based predictive models for intelligent energy management.
