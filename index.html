ABOUT THE PROJECT

Digital Twin and Data Fusion

This research project focuses on the integration of Digital Twin technology and multi-source data fusion for intelligent management of grid-integrated solar photovoltaic systems supporting electric vehicle charging infrastructure.

The project aims to develop an intelligent, data-driven framework that can improve renewable energy utilization, predictive management, grid stability, and efficient EV charging.
