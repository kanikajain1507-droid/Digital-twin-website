About the Project

The rapid adoption of Electric Vehicles (EVs) and the increasing integration of Solar Photovoltaic (PV) systems are transforming modern energy infrastructure. This research proposes an intelligent framework that combines Digital Twin Technology with Data Fusion to enhance the monitoring, optimization, and management of grid-connected renewable energy systems.

By continuously synchronizing data from solar panels, smart meters, battery storage, weather services, and EV charging stations, the Digital Twin enables predictive analytics, fault detection, and intelligent energy optimization, supporting the development of resilient, efficient, and sustainable smart grids.

PROJECT OBJECTIVES

Advancing Intelligent Energy Management through Digital Twin Technology

The project aims to develop an intelligent Digital Twin framework integrating Data Fusion, Artificial Intelligence, and Renewable Energy technologies to optimize Grid-Integrated Solar PV systems for Electric Vehicle charging.

01

Digital Twin Development

Develop a real-time Digital Twin capable of continuously synchronizing operational data with the physical Solar PV–EV charging infrastructure for monitoring, simulation and control.

02

Multi-source Data Fusion

Integrate sensor measurements, weather information, battery telemetry, smart meters and EV charging data into a unified operational model.

03

Renewable Energy Optimization

Maximize Solar PV utilization while minimizing dependence on conventional grid electricity through intelligent energy scheduling.

04

Predictive Analytics

Apply Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning to forecast solar generation, charging demand and system behaviour under dynamic operating conditions.

05

Grid Stability

Improve grid reliability by balancing renewable generation, battery storage and EV charging through intelligent optimization strategies.

06

Scalable Smart Energy Framework

Develop a scalable framework that can be deployed across future smart grid ecosystems supporting sustainable transportation and clean energy integration.
